Football Stardom privacy policy
Last updated: [9/29/2026]

Football Stardom is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device.

We do not collect any data. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.
Your saves stay on your device. Careers, the career archive, achievements and settings are stored only on your phone. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include them, under your backup settings.
Sharing is up to you. When you share a card, the game makes a picture and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice and is covered by the app you share it to.
Backup and restore shows your save data as text so you can copy it yourself. The game never sends it anywhere.
Children. The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
